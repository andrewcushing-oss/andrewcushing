# Andrew Cushing Consulting

> Technology consultant specializing in mobile commerce and international expansion. Based in San Francisco, CA.

Andrew Cushing is a technology consultant and former Director of Mobile Application Development at Levi Strauss & Co., where he spent 17 years leading digital commerce initiatives across North America, Europe, and beyond.
